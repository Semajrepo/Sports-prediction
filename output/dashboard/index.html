How the volleyball picks are made

  • Every morning the system saves yesterday's, today's and tomorrow's games from API-Volleyball, retrains, and makes picks for today and tomorrow (the free plan lists no further ahead).
  • Team strength comes from points won and lost in every set, recent games counting more, plus a league level so stronger leagues rate higher in the Champions League.
  • From rallies to sets: a side's chance of winning a rally gives its exact chance of winning a set to 25 (or the 5th set to 15), and from that every final score from 3-0 to 0-3.
  • Best pick: the safest option between 55% and 79% across winner, set handicap (±1.5, ±2.5), total sets (over/under 3.5 and 4.5) and 1st-set winner.
  • No bookmaker prices are available for volleyball from our free sources, so chances come from the ratings alone.
  • Coverage: Italy, Poland, Turkey, Brazil, Russia and Japan (men's and women's top leagues), Germany and France (men), and the CEV Champions League (men's; women's from this season).

How the picks are made

  • Every morning the system downloads the latest results and fixtures, retrains on every match played so far, and makes picks for today and the next two days. Games that have already kicked off never get picks.
  • Pick 1 (Result): a straight Home or Away win when it is at least 65% likely; otherwise the safer Home/Draw or Away/Draw.
  • Picks 2 and 3: the safest option in two different markets (goals, corners or cards), between 55% and 85% likely, with at most one corners and one cards pick per match.
  • Result and goal chances come from bookmakers' prices with their margin removed, turned into expected goals for each side. Corners and cards come from each team's record, adjusted for how strong each side is.
  • Settled on 90 minutes. Extra time and penalties never count. League results come from football-data.co.uk; cup and European results from API-Football, which reports the 90-minute score separately from extra time and penalties.
  • A match's picks are frozen on match day, so the record always shows the last prediction made before kickoff.

What the labels mean

Chance
How often picks like this have won. 80% means about 8 in 10.
Result
Home, Away, Home/Draw or Away/Draw.
Goals
Over/Under, GG/NG (both teams to score), team goals and 1st-half goals.
Corners
Total, home or away corners.
Cards
Total, home or away cards; yellows and reds count one each.
History agrees / split
Whether the same bet would have won in each team's last 8 matches and past meetings. Context only: it did not change how often picks won.
Live / Back-check / Test
Live: made before kickoff. Back-check: generated after the games from pre-match data. Test: from the backtest.

Coverage

Leagues: England (Premier League, Championship, League One, League Two), Spain, Italy, Germany, France, Netherlands, Portugal, Belgium, Turkey, Greece and Scotland.

Cups and Europe: Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, EFL Cup, FA Cup, DFB-Pokal, Copa del Rey, Coppa Italia and Coupe de France. These get Result and goals picks only. The Portuguese, Dutch, Belgian, Turkish, Greek and Scottish cups aren't available from our data sources.

Safe does not mean profitable: bookmakers' margins mean that even picks that win often can lose money over time, and SportyBet's prices are usually lower than other bookmakers'. Picks from the same match move together, so a same-match slip is riskier than its chance suggests.
